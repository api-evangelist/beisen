# Beisen

> Beisen (北森) is China's largest cloud-native Human Capital Management (HCM) and talent-management SaaS provider, delivered through its iTalent platform. Its integrated suite spans recruiting and applicant tracking, talent assessment, performance and goals (OKR), core HR, compensation, learning, and succession planning for large enterprises. Beisen operates an Open Platform (open.italent.cn) for integrating HR data and workflows into the iTalent ecosystem. Publicly listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (9669).

## Company
- [Website](https://www.beisen.com/): Beisen corporate website
- [Open Platform (Developer Portal)](https://open.italent.cn/): iTalent open platform for partner and customer integrations
